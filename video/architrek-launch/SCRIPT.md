# SCRIPT — architrek-launch

**Voice:** Kore (Gemini, gemini-3.8-flash-tts). Draft renders may use a Kokoro stand-in.
**Voice settings:** speed 1
**Voice direction:** Calm, clear and friendly, like a good lecturer showing a tool to students. Unhurried. A short pause between sentences.

---

## Line 1 — Hook (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 5.0s
**Delivery:** Curious, a little knowing. Land on "connect".

    Every ArchiMate student hits this question. How do these two boxes connect?

## Line 2 — The gap (Frame 2)

**Time:** 5.0 – 11.0s
**Delivery:** Matter of fact. Slight weight on "through what".

    The relationship table tells you what is allowed. It does not tell you through what.

## Line 3 — Product intro (Frame 3)

**Time:** 11.0 – 14.0s
**Delivery:** Confident and simple.

    ArchiTrek finds the route.

## Line 4 — Pick (Frame 4)

**Time:** 14.0 – 20.0s
**Delivery:** Instructional, easy pace.

    Pick a start and an end. Add a stop in between if you want one. Then click Find Path.

## Line 5 — Routes (Frame 5)

**Time:** 20.0 – 27.0s
**Delivery:** Steady.

    ArchiTrek searches the Appendix B tables and ranks every legal route. Direct relationships come first.

## Line 6 — Decide each hop (Frame 6)

**Time:** 27.0 – 33.0s
**Delivery:** Inviting.

    You decide each hop. Right-click an arrow to choose its relationship.

## Line 7 — Explanation (Frame 7)

**Time:** 33.0 – 39.0s
**Delivery:** Warm. Weight on "why".

    Then read why. Every hop comes with its rule and its section in the specification.

## Line 8 — Rigor (Frame 8)

**Time:** 39.0 – 44.0s
**Delivery:** Light.

    Keep the search strict for your assignments, or loosen it to explore.

## Line 9 — Export (Frame 9)

**Time:** 44.0 – 50.0s
**Delivery:** Practical.

    Take the route with you, as a picture, a share link, or a model that opens in Archi.

## Line 10 — Close (Frame 10)

**Time:** 50.0 – 55.0s
**Delivery:** Warm sign-off.

    ArchiTrek. Free, and it runs in your browser.
